Frequently asked questions

Is my webcam video uploaded anywhere?

No. Face analysis runs frame by frame and only numbers (focus %, blink rate, head pose) are stored. Video and audio are never saved.

How does the AI speech coach measure confidence?

It transcribes your speech locally with Whisper and scores pace, filler words, pauses, pronunciation clarity, pitch variety, hedging language and eye contact, then gives specific tips and a more confident rewrite of what you said.

How is fatigue predicted?

A scikit-learn model uses session length, time of day, drowsy and distracted time, blink rate, yawns and eye openness. It improves as you rate how tired you felt after each session.

How do I sign up?

Create an account with your email and mobile number and confirm both with one-time codes. After that you can sign in with a password or a one-time code.
